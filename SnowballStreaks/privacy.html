Snowball Streaks

Privacy Policy

Snowball Streaks is designed to be lightweight and local-first.

Data we collect

Snowball Streaks does not require account creation and does not intentionally collect personal account data for core app use.

How data is used

  • Habit, streak, timer, and history data are stored locally on your device.
  • App Group storage is used so widgets can display your latest progress.
  • Notification permission is used only for habit reminders if you enable reminders.
  • Siri / App Intents are used only when you actively trigger shortcuts.

Third parties

Based on the current app setup, Snowball Streaks does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Your choices

  • You can disable reminders in the app or in iPhone Settings.
  • You can remove the app to delete local data stored on your device.
