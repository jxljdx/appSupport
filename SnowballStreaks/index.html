App Support

Snowball Streaks helps you build better habits with small daily actions, streak tracking, focus timers, widgets, and gentle progress history.

Use this page as your App Store Support URL. If you publish it publicly, update the contact section below with your preferred support email or website contact form.

Need help?

  • Make sure notifications are enabled in iPhone Settings if reminders do not fire.
  • If widgets look empty, remove and re-add the widget after opening the app once.
  • If Siri or Shortcuts do not appear, reopen the app and try the shortcut again after the app has launched fully.

Frequently asked questions

  • Does the app require an account? No. Snowball Streaks is designed to work without account signup.
  • Where is my data stored? Habit data is stored locally on your device, with App Group sharing used for widgets.
  • Why don’t I see the Live Activity? Live Activities only appear while a timer is actively running and if system support is available on your device.
  • Can I change language? Yes. Change the app language from Settings inside the app.
