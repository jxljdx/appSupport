Deposit Shield

Privacy Policy

Deposit Shield is designed to help renters stay organized without requiring an account.

Data we collect

Deposit Shield does not require account creation and does not intentionally collect personal account data for core app use.

How data is used

  • Property records, room details, evidence notes, and communication proof are stored locally on your device.
  • Imported photos and screenshots are used only to help you document move-in or move-out condition and keep related proof together.
  • Notification permission is used only if you choose to enable lease-end or walkthrough reminders.
  • App preferences such as reminder settings and Pro status are stored locally on your device.

Third parties

Based on the current app setup, Deposit Shield does not use third-party advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs.

If you purchase Pro, payment is handled by Apple through StoreKit and your Apple account.

Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke Photos or Notifications permissions in iPhone Settings at any time.
  • You can delete records inside the app or remove the app to delete local data stored on your device.
  • You can choose what evidence, screenshots, and notes to save for each property.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact:

Email: support@depositshield.app

Deposit Shield

隐私政策

Deposit Shield 旨在帮助租客整理证据和记录，核心功能无需注册账号。

我们收集的数据

Deposit Shield 不要求注册账号，核心使用流程也不会主动收集账号类个人数据。

数据如何使用

  • 房源记录、房间信息、证据备注和沟通截图默认保存在你的设备本地。
  • 你主动导入的照片和截图只用于记录入住或退租状态，并把相关证据整理到一起。
  • 通知权限只会在你主动开启租约结束或 final walkthrough 提醒时使用。
  • 提醒设置、Pro 状态等应用偏好默认保存在你的设备本地。

第三方服务

基于当前 app 结构，Deposit Shield 不使用第三方广告 SDK，也不使用第三方分析统计 SDK。

如果你购买 Pro，支付流程由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 和你的 Apple 账户处理。

你的选择

  • 你可以随时在 iPhone 系统设置中拒绝或撤销照片、通知权限。
  • 你可以在 app 内删除记录，或直接删除 app 来移除设备上的本地数据。
  • 你可以自行决定每个房源保存哪些照片、截图和备注。

联系我们

如果你有隐私或支持相关问题，请联系：

邮箱：support@depositshield.app
