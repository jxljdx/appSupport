Deposit Shield

App Support

Deposit Shield helps renters organize move-in and move-out proof, keep room photos and issue evidence together, and stay on top of lease-end reminders before deposit disputes happen.

Use this page as your App Store Support URL.

Need help?

  • If photos or screenshots do not import, reopen Photos access in iPhone Settings and try again.
  • If reminders do not appear, confirm Notifications are enabled for Deposit Shield and that the property has a lease end date.
  • If Pro does not unlock after purchase, open Settings and use Restore Purchases.

Support contact

Contact us if you need help using Deposit Shield.

Email: support@depositshield.app

Response time: usually within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Deposit Shield works without account signup.
  • Where is my data stored? Property records, room notes, evidence, and communication proof are stored locally on your device.
  • What counts as proof? You can save room photos, issue evidence, and communication screenshots so they stay attached to the right property.
  • How do reminders work? Deposit Shield can schedule local notifications before lease end and walkthrough dates when you enable reminders.

常见问题

  • 需要注册账号吗？不需要。Deposit Shield 无需账号即可使用。
  • 数据存在哪里？房源记录、房间备注、证据照片和沟通截图默认保存在你的设备本地。
  • 哪些内容算证据？你可以保存房间照片、问题证据和沟通截图，并把它们归档到对应房源下。
  • 提醒是怎么工作的？当你开启提醒后，Deposit Shield 可以在租约结束或 final walkthrough 前发送本地通知。
