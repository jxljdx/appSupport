Frequently asked questions

Does Batchly upload my photos or videos?

No. Photo and video processing happens on your device. Your media, filenames, and batch projects are not uploaded by Batchly.

Where are processed files saved?

You can save processed copies to Photos or share the exported files. If you enable ZIP packaging, Batchly creates one archive for sharing. Temporary outputs are removed when you leave the export results.

Will Batchly delete my originals automatically?

No. Batchly saves successful copies first. It only requests deletion of eligible originals after you choose the delete option and confirm the separate iOS Photos prompt. Deleted items remain in Recently Deleted until removed there.

Why can some originals not be deleted?

Batchly can only request deletion for media currently available through your Photos permission. With Limited Photos access, items outside the permitted selection remain unavailable.

What are the free daily limits?

Single-photo processing is unlimited. Free multi-photo processing includes up to 5 successfully processed photos per day. Free video compression includes up to 10 minutes of successfully compressed source video per day. Batchly Pro removes both daily limits.

How do I restore Batchly Pro?

Open Batchly Pro in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Why is my compressed video still large?

Output size depends on source duration, resolution, motion, codec, and the quality option you select. Choose a smaller quality preset or lower resolution when file size is the priority.

Can Batchly remove private photo details?

Batchly Pro can remove location metadata or broader camera and device details from processed copies. Always review the exported copy before sharing.

常见问题

Batchly 会上传我的照片或视频吗？

不会。照片和视频处理都在你的设备本地完成。Batchly 不会上传你的媒体、文件名或批处理项目。

处理后的文件保存在哪里？

你可以把处理后的副本保存到“照片”，也可以直接分享导出文件。开启 ZIP 打包后，Batchly 会创建一个压缩包用于分享。离开导出结果页后，临时输出会被移除。

Batchly 会自动删除原件吗？

不会。Batchly 会先保存成功处理的副本。只有当你选择删除并再次确认 iOS 的“照片”系统提示后，才会请求删除符合条件的原件。删除的项目仍会保留在“最近删除”中，直到你在那里彻底移除。

为什么有些原件无法删除？

Batchly 只能请求删除当前“照片”权限允许访问的媒体。如果使用“有限访问”，未加入允许范围的项目将不可用。

免费版每天有哪些额度？

单张照片处理不限次数。多张照片批处理每天可成功处理最多 5 张照片；免费视频压缩每天包含最多 10 分钟成功压缩的源视频时长。Batchly Pro 会移除这两项每日额度。

如何恢复 Batchly Pro？

在 App 中打开 Batchly Pro，然后点按恢复购买。请使用购买时的同一个 Apple 账户。你可以在 App Store 账户设置中管理或取消订阅。

为什么压缩后的视频仍然很大？

输出大小取决于源视频时长、分辨率、画面运动、编码格式和所选画质。如果更看重文件大小，请选择更小的画质预设或更低的分辨率。

Batchly 可以移除照片中的隐私信息吗？

Batchly Pro 可以从处理后的副本中移除位置元数据，或进一步移除相机和设备信息。分享前请检查导出的副本。
