Privacy Policy

Batchly is built around on-device media processing and gives you control over what is selected, exported, shared, or deleted.

Overview

Batchly does not require an account. The app does not upload your photos, videos, filenames, batch projects, contact information, or device identifiers for media processing or feedback.

This policy explains the data Batchly accesses, where processing occurs, and the limited circumstances in which information is sent to Apple services.

Photos and videos

  • Batchly requests Photos access so you can select media, read the selected originals, save processed copies, and optionally request deletion of originals.
  • Photo and video compression, resizing, conversion, renaming, metadata changes, and ZIP creation happen locally on your device.
  • Your media is not sent to Batchly servers.
  • Batchly creates temporary processing files on your device. Temporary export files are removed when you leave the relevant results screen, and abandoned sessions are cleaned up when the app starts.
  • Batchly never deletes originals automatically. Deletion is requested only after successful copies are saved, you choose the delete action, and you confirm Apple's separate system prompt.

Data stored on your device

  • Batch project references, processing recipes, saved presets, and preferences are stored locally in the app's container.
  • Daily free-usage counters for photo batches and video duration are stored locally using system preferences.
  • Deleting a batch project inside Batchly removes Batchly's references and settings; it does not delete media from Apple Photos.
  • Removing the app deletes data stored in the app's local container, subject to standard iOS behavior.

Optional feedback

If you choose to send feedback from Settings, Batchly sends the following information to an Apple CloudKit public database:

  • the feedback category and message you enter;
  • the Batchly version and build number;
  • the iOS system version; and
  • the submission date and time.

Do not include sensitive or personal information in feedback. Batchly does not collect your email address or other contact details with in-app feedback and therefore cannot reply to it. Apple may attach Apple-managed account, device, network, or service metadata under Apple's own privacy policies.

Purchases and third-party services

  • Batchly Pro purchases and subscription restoration are processed by Apple through StoreKit and your Apple Account. Batchly receives entitlement status but does not receive your full payment details.
  • Batchly does not include third-party advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Apple services used by Batchly, including Photos, StoreKit, and CloudKit, are governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy policy.

Your choices

  • You can deny, limit, or revoke Photos access at any time in iPhone Settings.
  • You decide whether to save or share processed outputs and whether to request deletion of originals.
  • You can delete Batchly projects without affecting media in Photos.
  • You can choose not to send in-app feedback.
  • You can manage or cancel Batchly Pro subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Children's privacy

Batchly is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when Batchly's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions, email jxstudio.apps@gmail.com.

隐私政策

Batchly 坚持在设备本地处理媒体，并让你自主决定选择、导出、分享或删除哪些内容。

概述

Batchly 不要求注册账号。进行媒体处理或发送反馈时，App 不会把你的照片、视频、文件名、批处理项目、联系信息或设备标识符上传到 Batchly 的服务器。

本政策说明 Batchly 会访问哪些数据、处理在哪里完成，以及哪些有限信息可能被发送到 Apple 服务。

照片与视频

  • Batchly 请求“照片”权限，以便你选择媒体、读取所选原件、保存处理后的副本，并可选择请求删除原件。
  • 照片和视频压缩、缩放、转换、重命名、元数据调整和 ZIP 创建均在你的设备本地完成。
  • 你的媒体不会被发送到 Batchly 的服务器。
  • Batchly 会在设备上创建临时处理文件。离开相关结果页面后，临时导出文件会被移除；App 启动时也会清理遗留的处理会话。
  • Batchly 不会自动删除原件。只有在成功保存副本、你主动选择删除，并确认 Apple 单独显示的系统提示后，才会请求删除原件。

保存在设备上的数据

  • 批处理项目引用、处理方案、已保存预设和偏好设置保存在 App 的本地容器中。
  • 照片批处理和视频时长的每日免费使用计数通过系统偏好设置保存在本地。
  • 在 Batchly 中删除批处理项目只会移除 Batchly 的引用和设置，不会删除 Apple“照片”中的媒体。
  • 删除 App 会移除其本地容器中的数据，具体遵循 iOS 的标准行为。

可选的应用内反馈

如果你选择从“设置”发送反馈，Batchly 会把以下信息发送到 Apple CloudKit 公共数据库：

  • 你选择的反馈类型和输入的反馈内容；
  • Batchly 的版本和构建号；
  • iOS 系统版本；以及
  • 提交日期和时间。

请勿在反馈中填写敏感或个人信息。应用内反馈不会收集你的邮箱地址或其他联系信息，因此 Batchly 无法直接回复。Apple 可能依据其自身隐私政策附加由 Apple 管理的账户、设备、网络或服务元数据。

购买与第三方服务

  • Batchly Pro 的购买和恢复订阅由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 和你的 Apple 账户处理。Batchly 会收到权益状态，但不会获取你的完整付款信息。
  • Batchly 不包含第三方广告 SDK，也不包含第三方分析统计 SDK。
  • Batchly 使用的 Apple 服务（包括“照片”、StoreKit 和 CloudKit）受 Apple 适用条款及隐私政策约束。

你的选择

  • 你可以随时在 iPhone 系统设置中拒绝、限制或撤销“照片”权限。
  • 你可以自主决定是否保存或分享处理结果，以及是否请求删除原件。
  • 你可以删除 Batchly 项目，而不影响“照片”中的媒体。
  • 你可以选择不发送应用内反馈。
  • 你可以在 App Store 账户设置中管理或取消 Batchly Pro 订阅。

儿童隐私

Batchly 是一款通用工具，并非面向儿童设计，也不会在知情的情况下收集儿童的个人信息。

政策更新

当 Batchly 的功能或数据处理方式发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策。页面顶部的生效日期代表最新版本。
