Privacy policy · Prepared November 11, 2026

On-device photo processing

DepthPop does not require an account or provide a photo-upload service. Depth prediction, photo-layer preparation, rendering and export run on your iPhone. The app includes no advertising SDK or analytics SDK and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Local projects and temporary files

A saved project contains the imported image, relative depth, prepared photo layers, edits, title and source credit inside the app. Projects are excluded from device backups and do not sync between devices. Preferences and free-usage counters are also stored locally. Temporary export files are created in the app's cache.

Deleting a project removes its app-managed files, not your original in Apple Photos. Deleting the app removes its local projects. Export important work separately; DepthPop does not offer account-based recovery.

Photos, camera and motion

  • Choosing a photo uses Apple's system picker. A selected iCloud photo may first download through Apple Photos under your own settings.
  • Camera access is requested when you choose to take a photo.
  • Motion input is used while the editor's tilt preview is active; it is not uploaded.
  • Saving to Photos requests Add Photos access. You may deny or revoke permissions in iPhone Settings.

Exports and sharing

Exported media do not copy source GPS metadata. You decide whether to save an output to Photos or share it through another app. Apple Photos, iCloud and your chosen sharing destinations handle those copies under their own settings and policies. DepthPop's local-processing statement does not mean these separate services never use a network.

Purchases

Apple processes Pro subscriptions and restoration through StoreKit and your Apple Account. DepthPop checks verified entitlement information to unlock features, but does not receive your payment-card details. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Optional support contact

If you email support, we receive the email address, message and any information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to the request and meet applicable obligations. Do not include private photos, passwords or payment details. The app does not automatically transmit projects, photos or diagnostic files to support.

For a support or privacy request, contact jxstudio.apps@gmail.com. Information you send by email is separate from the app's local photo processing.

This support website

The pages use a browser-local language preference, which you may clear in your browser. The DepthPop page code contains no analytics or advertising script. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information needed to serve the site, under its own policy. Visiting this site does not give it access to your DepthPop projects.

Your choices and policy changes

You can keep using free features without subscribing, decline permissions, delete local projects and choose not to contact support. DepthPop is a general-purpose photo app, not a service directed to children. Changes to this policy will be reflected on this page.

隐私政策 · 编制于 2026 年 11 月 11 日

本地照片处理

DepthPop 无需注册账户，也不提供照片上传服务。深度预测、照片分层、渲染和导出都在 iPhone 本地进行。App 不包含广告或分析 SDK，不会跨其他 App 或网站追踪你。

本地项目和临时文件

保存的项目在 App 内包含导入图像、相对深度、已准备的照片分层、编辑参数、标题和来源信息。项目不进入设备备份，也不跨设备同步。偏好和免费使用次数同样保存在本机；临时导出文件位于 App 缓存。

删除项目仅移除 App 管理的文件，不会删除 Apple 照片中的原图。删除 App 会移除本地项目。重要作品请另行导出保存；DepthPop 不提供基于账户的恢复服务。

照片、相机和运动

  • 选择照片使用 Apple 系统选择器。所选 iCloud 照片可能按你自己的设置先由 Apple 照片下载。
  • 只有选择拍摄时才申请相机权限。
  • 运动输入只在编辑器的倾斜预览启用时使用，不会上传。
  • 保存到照片时申请“添加照片”权限。你可以在 iPhone 设置中拒绝或撤销权限。

导出和分享

导出媒体不会复制原图 GPS 信息。是否保存到照片或通过其他 App 分享由你决定。Apple 照片、iCloud 和分享目标按照各自的设置与政策处理这些副本。“本地处理”不表示这些独立服务绝不使用网络。

购买

Apple 通过 StoreKit 和 Apple 账户处理 Pro 订阅与恢复。DepthPop 检查已验证的权益信息以解锁功能，但不会收到支付卡详情。你可以在 Apple 账户设置中管理或取消订阅。

自愿联系支持

如果发送支持邮件，我们会收到邮件地址、正文及你主动提供的信息。这些信息用于回复请求和履行适用义务。请勿包含私人照片、密码或付款详情。App 不会自动向支持渠道传送项目、照片或诊断文件。

如有支持或隐私问题，请联系 jxstudio.apps@gmail.com。你通过邮件发送的信息与 App 本地照片处理是不同的渠道。

本支持网站

网页在浏览器本地记住语言偏好，你可以在浏览器中清除。DepthPop 网页代码没有分析或广告脚本。托管服务商可能按其政策处理提供网页所需的普通请求信息。访问本网站不会授予它读取 DepthPop 项目的权限。

你的选择和政策更新

你可以不订阅而继续使用免费功能、拒绝权限、删除本地项目，以及不发送支持邮件。DepthPop 是通用照片 App，不是专门面向儿童的服务。政策变更将体现在本页面。
