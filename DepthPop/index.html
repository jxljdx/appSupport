DepthPop

A little help
for your moments.

DepthPop turns a still photo into a small-angle 2.5D parallax scene. Photo processing runs on your iPhone, without a photo-upload service.

Make your first moving photo

  1. Choose a photo, take a photo, or try a sample. Allow processing to finish.
  2. Compare Original and Parallax. Drag the photo or tap Play to explore it.
  3. Choose Gentle or Bold. If the cutout looks wrong, try Whole photo.
  4. Tap Done to save a local project, or Export to create a video or Live Photo. Save to Photos or share the result.

Small movements work best. This is not a 3D scan, and the estimated background is not a recovery of hidden detail.

Common questions

Why do hair, petals or pets look imperfect?

A single photo can have uncertain depth and cutout boundaries. Fine hair, fur, glass, transparent objects and blank backgrounds are difficult. Try Gentle or Whole photo, or a photo with clearer layers. Changing the mode does not guarantee a correct cutout.

Where are my projects?

Projects stay in this app on this iPhone. They do not sync between devices and are excluded from device backups. Deleting the app removes its projects. Keep exported copies of moments you want to retain; unsaved edits are not promised to survive closing the app.

What is included for free?

You can keep up to five local projects and complete three free exports per local day. Standard video and Live Photo exports use 720p-class output with a small DepthPop watermark. Failed or canceled exports do not use an export allowance.

What does Pro include?

Pro adds unlimited exports without the watermark, output up to 4K, finer depth controls, depth-map export and more local projects. Monthly, three-month, six-month and yearly auto-renewing plans are offered. There is no free trial. Apple shows the current local price and billing period before you confirm.

How do I restore or cancel Pro?

Open Settings in DepthPop and choose Restore purchases using the Apple Account that purchased Pro. Manage or cancel renewal in your Apple Account subscriptions. Canceling renewal does not itself request a refund.

Why does sharing a Live Photo send a video?

Use Save to Photos to preserve the Live Photo image-and-motion pair. The app's share action sends its motion video because other apps may not support Live Photos. Exported loops are silent.

Why can I not save or use Tilt?

Saving requires Add Photos permission. Camera access is requested only when you take a photo. Tilt needs an available motion sensor and is disabled by Reduce Motion; drag remains available. Check the relevant iPhone permissions and keep enough storage available.

留住美好，
也用得明白。

DepthPop 将静态照片变为小角度的 2.5D 视差画面。照片处理在 iPhone 本地完成，App 不提供照片上传服务。

制作第一张动态照片

  1. 选择照片、拍摄照片或试用示例，等待处理完成。
  2. 对比“原图”和“视差”，拖动照片或点“播放”查看效果。
  3. 选择“轻柔”或“明显”。抠图不合适时，可试“整图”。
  4. 点“完成”保存本地项目，或点“导出”创建视频、实况照片，再保存到照片或分享。

小幅运动通常更自然。这不是三维扫描，估计出的背景也不是被遮挡细节的真实还原。

常见问题

头发、花瓣或宠物边缘为什么不自然？

单张照片的深度和抠图边界可能不准确。细发、毛发、玻璃、透明物体和空白背景尤其困难。可以试“轻柔”“整图”，或层次更清楚的照片；切换模式不保证修好抠图。

项目保存在哪里？

项目只在这台 iPhone 的 App 内保存，不跨设备同步，也不进入设备备份。删除 App 会移除项目。重要作品请另留导出副本；未保存的编辑不保证在关闭 App 后保留。

免费版包含什么？

最多保留五个本地项目，每个本地自然日可成功导出三次。标准视频和实况照片使用 720p 级别输出，并带有小型 DepthPop 水印。失败或取消的导出不扣次数。

Pro 有哪些功能？

Pro 支持无限导出、去水印、最高 4K 输出、更细致的深度控制、深度图导出和更多本地项目。可选择月度、季度、半年或年度自动续订，无免费试用。确认购买前，Apple 会显示当前地区的价格与计费周期。

如何恢复或取消 Pro？

在 DepthPop 设置中选择“恢复购买”，并使用购买时的 Apple 账户。可在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中管理或取消续订。取消续订本身不等于申请退款。

分享实况照片为什么变成视频？

使用“保存到照片”可保留实况照片的图像与动态配对。其他 App 未必支持实况照片，因此 App 的分享操作发送动态视频。导出的循环没有声音。

为什么不能保存或使用倾斜？

保存需要“添加照片”权限；仅在拍摄时申请相机权限。倾斜需要可用的运动传感器，开启“减弱动态效果”时会停用，但仍可拖动。请检查相应 iPhone 权限，并保留足够存储空间。
