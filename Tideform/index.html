Tideform · 时塑

A little help
for your dates.

Tideform gives a countdown a living flower. Growth follows your saved dates, not how often you open the app.

Start with a date

Add a title and target date. Choose a flower; its catalogue shows the full-bloom style, while your actual flower grows by local calendar day. Preview growth does not change your dates.

Past dates stay in bloom. Annual and monthly events restart only after you confirm Start next cycle. Long-press a Home flower or specimen, or open More in its detail, to pin one upcoming date. Its pin clears after the target day.

Reminders, widgets and local data

Reminders are optional. Allow notifications and open Tideform occasionally to extend the displayed scheduling coverage. iOS controls delivery and widget refresh timing. Repeating reminders do not restart a flower.

The small Home Screen widget shows a saved date. Pinning Home does not change the widget's selection. Your dates stay on this device; there is no Tideform account or event synchronization. Device backups are controlled by iOS.

Generated date images include the title and dates, not the private note. Sharing is explicit. Keep copies you want to retain before deleting app data.

Free and Pro

Free includes three saved dates (including kept dates), Pink bloom, Tulip and Daisy. Home pinning, reminders, all widget configuration/display, and date-image generation and sharing are free.

Pro adds unlimited saved dates and all ten flowers. Monthly, three-month, six-month and yearly auto-renewing plans have the same benefits and no free trial. Apple shows the current local price and full billing period before you confirm.

After expiry, all saved dates remain viewable, editable and deletable, including their existing flowers. Reminders, widgets and image generation/sharing keep working, including images of saved premium flowers. Only new dates beyond the free allowance and changing to a different premium flower require Pro.

Open Tideform Pro to restore purchases with the Apple Account that subscribed. Restoring purchases does not restore local dates. Canceling renewal keeps access until the paid period ends.

留住期待，
也用得明白。

时塑让倒计时慢慢长成一朵花。生长取决于保存的日期，不取决于你打开 App 的次数。

从一个日子开始

填写名称和目标日期，再选择花形。花形目录展示盛开样式，实际花朵按本地自然日生长。“预览生长”不会更改日期。

过去的日子保持盛开。年度、月度日子仅在确认“开启下一轮”后重新生长。长按主页的大花或小花，或打开详情“更多”，可置顶一个将至日子；目标日过后自动取消置顶。

提醒、小组件与本地数据

提醒由你选择开启。请允许通知，并偶尔打开时塑以补充后续提醒，详情会显示已安排到的日期。通知送达和小组件刷新时机由 iOS 控制。重复提醒不会重启花朵生长。

小号主屏幕小组件显示已保存的日子。主页置顶不会改变小组件选择。日子保存在本机，不提供时塑账号或日子同步；设备备份由 iOS 管理。

生成的日子图片包含名称和日期，不包含私人寄语。仅在你主动操作后分享。删除 App 数据前，请另留需要保留的图片副本。

免费版与 Pro

免费版可保存三个日子（含已珍藏日子），使用粉色绽放、郁金香和雏菊。主页置顶、提醒、全部小组件配置与显示，以及日子图片生成与分享均免费。

Pro 可保存不限数量的日子，使用全部十种花形。月度、季度、半年和年度自动续订享有相同权益，无免费试用。确认购买前，Apple 会显示当前地区价格及完整计费周期。

到期后，已保存日子及原有花形仍可查看、编辑和删除。提醒、小组件及图片生成与分享仍免费，包括已保存的付费花形。仅超出免费数量的新建及更换另一种付费花形需要 Pro。

可在“时塑 Pro”中使用订阅时的 Apple 账户恢复购买。恢复购买不会恢复本地日子。取消续订后，可使用至已付费周期结束。
