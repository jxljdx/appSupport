What you receive

Pro provides unlimited saved dates and all ten flowers while active. Monthly, three-month, six-month and yearly subscriptions have the same benefits. There is no free trial. The actual localized total price and billing period appear in the app and Apple's confirmation sheet.

Free includes three saved dates (including kept dates), Pink bloom, Tulip and Daisy. Pinning, reminders, widget configuration/display, and date-image generation and sharing do not require Pro.

Billing, cancellation and expiry

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel renewal through your Apple Account subscriptions.

Canceling renewal retains access through the paid period; it is not a refund request. After expiry, existing dates remain viewable, editable and deletable with their saved flowers. Reminders, widgets and image generation/sharing continue for free, including images of saved premium flowers. Pro is needed only to add beyond the free allowance or choose a different premium flower. Images already generated are not removed merely because access expires.

Restore purchases in Tideform Pro with the Apple Account that subscribed. Subscription rights and local event data are separate: restoring purchases does not synchronize or restore dates.

The Apple Standard EULA applies. Manage subscriptions.

订阅权益

Pro 有效期内可保存不限数量的日子、使用全部十种花形。月度、季度、半年、年度享有相同权益，无免费试用。实际本地总价及计费周期将在 App 和 Apple 确认页面显示。

免费版可保存三个日子（含已珍藏日子），使用粉色绽放、郁金香和雏菊。置顶、提醒、小组件配置与显示、日子图片生成与分享均不需要 Pro。

扣费、取消与到期

确认购买后，费用从 Apple 账户扣除。如未在当前周期结束至少 24 小时前取消，订阅将自动续订，并在周期结束前 24 小时内收取续订费用。可在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中管理或取消续订。

取消续订后，可使用至已付费周期结束；取消续订不等于申请退款。到期后，已有日子及其花形仍可查看、编辑和删除。提醒、小组件及图片生成与分享继续免费，包括已保存的付费花形。仅超出免费数量的新建及更换另一种付费花形需要 Pro。已生成图片不会仅因订阅到期而移除。

可在“时塑 Pro”中使用订阅时的 Apple 账户恢复购买。订阅权益与本地日子数据分开，恢复购买不会同步或恢复日子。
