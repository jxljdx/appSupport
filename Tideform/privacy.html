Privacy policy · November 19, 2026

What Tideform stores

Event titles, dates, flowers, private notes, reminder choices, drafts, recovery records and your Home pin stay in the app's local storage. Tideform has no account system, event-upload service, analytics SDK, advertising or cross-app tracking.

The widget reads a local display snapshot shared with the app: event titles, dates and flowers, not notes, drafts or recovery records. Reminder requests go to iOS locally after you choose a reminder and grant permission. Titles may appear on the Lock Screen according to your notification settings.

Purchases and sharing

Apple processes payments, purchase history and subscription management. Tideform uses verified StoreKit transactions and subscription status to enable Pro. We do not receive payment card details. Apple's services may communicate with Apple; local event storage is not a claim that purchases are offline.

Generated date images contain the title and dates, not the private note. Nothing is sent to a sharing destination until you choose it. That destination handles its copy under its own policy. Tideform does not automatically save images to Photos or request access to your photo library.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete dates and disable reminders. Removing a date removes it from the local collection and reconciles its app-owned reminder requests and widget display. Deleting the app removes its local data. Shared copies and copies retained in iOS/device backups are separate; device backups are controlled by iOS. Tideform does not synchronize dates between devices.

Contacting support is optional. Your email provider and ours process your message and any information you choose to include. Do not send private notes, passwords or payment details. Contact jxstudio.apps@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests concerning support correspondence. Local event data is not accessible to us.

Material policy changes will be reflected here and in the app. This policy does not replace Apple's or a sharing recipient's policy.

隐私政策 · 2026 年 11 月 19 日

时塑保存哪些信息

日子名称、日期、花形、私人寄语、提醒选择、草稿、恢复记录及主页置顶保存在 App 本地。时塑不提供账号系统、日子上传服务、统计 SDK、广告或跨 App 跟踪。

小组件读取与 App 本地共享的显示快照，包含名称、日期和花形，不包含寄语、草稿或恢复记录。选择提醒并授权后，提醒请求会交给 iOS 在本地处理。名称可能按你的通知设置出现在锁定屏幕上。

购买与分享

付款、购买记录及订阅管理由 Apple 处理。时塑使用经过验证的 StoreKit 交易及订阅状态开通 Pro，不会收到银行卡详情。Apple 服务可能连接 Apple；本地保存日子并不代表购买可离线完成。

生成的日子图片包含名称和日期，不包含私人寄语。仅在你选择目标后才会分享，接收方将按其自身政策处理副本。时塑不会自动保存图片到照片，也不申请读取照片图库权限。

选择与删除

你可以编辑或删除日子、关闭提醒。删除日子会将其移出本地集合，并更新对应的 App 提醒请求和小组件显示。删除 App 会移除其本地数据。已分享副本及 iOS／设备备份中的副本独立存在，设备备份由 iOS 管理。时塑不跨设备同步日子。

联系支持由你自愿选择。你和我们的邮件服务商会处理邮件及你主动提供的信息。请勿发送私人寄语、密码或付款信息。隐私问题或有关支持邮件的请求请联系 jxstudio.apps@gmail.com。我们无法访问你的本地日子数据。

重要政策变更将更新在此页面及 App 内。本政策不替代 Apple 或分享接收方的政策。
